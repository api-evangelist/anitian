It’s been a tough couple of years for cybersecurity strategists and practitioners. In 2021, supply chain attacks on Kaseya, Solar Winds, Accellion and other hardware and software providers sowed doubt into their long-standing assumptions of trust. Ransomware piggybacked on some of these exploits to drive their severity and urgency (see Kaseya, again).
